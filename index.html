Compliance & Guidelines

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Please read the official rules governing eligibility, participation, and prize fulfillment.

Participation in the AURA 2026 Lucky Draw Campaign is open to individuals aged 18 years or older at the time of entry. Employees, affiliates, and direct family members of the organizing entity are not eligible to claim major tiered prizes.

To participate, eligible entrants must accurately complete the official registration form with their full name and verified 10-digit mobile number. Each participant receives a unique ticket ID granting one (1) interactive lucky draw turn.

Each participant is entitled to exactly one spin of the official prize wheel per registered mobile number. Any attempts to manipulate participation through automated bots or scripts will result in immediate disqualification.

Prizes are distributed strictly as announced. Physical items (Grand Prize, Premium Gift, Special Gift) will be dispatched within 14 business days following successful identity verification. Digital vouchers are delivered instantaneously upon redemption.

Winners are determined through an automated, cryptographically secure pseudo-random algorithm based on pre-defined probability weightings. The result rendered upon the conclusion of the wheel rotation is final and binding.

Winners must claim their prize within 30 days of the draw date by submitting their unique claim voucher code and government-issued ID. Unclaimed rewards will be reallocated to the Grand Finale community raffle pool.

The organizer reserves the right to void entries and withhold prizes if an entrant provides false or misleading information, violates terms of service, or attempts to circumvent draw rate limits.

Participant personal information is collected solely for the administration of this campaign, winner notification, and prize fulfillment. Your data is encrypted and never sold or shared with unauthorized third-party advertisers.

The organizers reserve the right to amend, suspend, or terminate the campaign at any time due to unforeseen technical difficulties or force majeure events. All applicable local taxes and duties on prizes are the sole responsibility of the winner.
